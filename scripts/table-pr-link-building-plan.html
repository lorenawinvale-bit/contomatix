A step-by-step PR link plan

Our working method for a small team. Times are estimates.

StepWhat you doTime
1. Find a storyPick data, a clear opinion or a timely angle1 week
2. Build the proofRun the study or write the piece, with sources1 to 3 weeks
3. Build a media listList writers who cover the topic, with recent articles2 days
4. Pitch in small wavesSend 5 to 10 personal emails, then one follow-up1 week
5. Track and recordLog live links, their rel and the coverageOngoing
