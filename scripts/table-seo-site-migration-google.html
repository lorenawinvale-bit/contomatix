Google's site move rules, in short

From the site move with URL changes guide in Google Search Central, read in English on 26 October 2026.

TopicWhat Google says
Redirect typeServer-side permanent redirects such as 301 and 308
ChainsAvoid chaining. Googlebot can follow up to 10 hops, but redirect to the final destination
PageRank301 and other permanent redirects don't cause a loss in PageRank
Small or medium sitesMove all URLs at once
Large sitesMay move one section at a time
Several changesDo them one at a time, such as domain first and layout later
How long to keep redirectsAs long as possible, generally at least 1 year
