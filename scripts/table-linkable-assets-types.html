Types of linkable assets compared

A working comparison from Contomatix. Effort is relative to a small team.

Asset typeWhy people cite itEffort
Free tool or calculatorDoes a job; easy to link as a referenceHigh, then low
Original data or studyA fact others can quote with a sourceHigh
Template or checklistSaves readers timeMedium
Glossary or definition pageA clear, neutral referenceMedium
Infographic or chartMakes data visual; needs an article beside itMedium
Resource hubOne place for a topicHigh
