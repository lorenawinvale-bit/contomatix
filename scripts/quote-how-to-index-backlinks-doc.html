What Google says about requesting indexing

You can't request indexing for URLs that you don't manage. Crawling can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks. Requesting a crawl does not guarantee that inclusion in search results will happen instantly or even at all.

Keep in mind that there's a quota for submitting individual URLs and requesting a recrawl multiple times for the same URL won't get it crawled any faster.
