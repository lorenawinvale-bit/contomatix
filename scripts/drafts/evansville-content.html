Search for the top recruiting companies in Evansville and the results skew heavily toward national brand names with a branch somewhere in the Tri-State area, alongside a handful of AI-generated directory summaries that list businesses no one can actually find a phone number for. Neither is especially useful if what you want is a staffing partner who understands that Evansville hires differently than Indianapolis or Louisville — more manufacturing floor, more skilled trades, more healthcare, less pure office work.

Roughly twenty-three recruiting and staffing firms serving the Evansville area were checked against one standard: is this a genuinely local operation, independently verifiable through public records, with a real, staffed office an employer could walk into? Six cleared it. All six are locally owned, though one operates under a national franchise brand — disclosed in full in its own profile below.

Why Evansville's Recruiting Market Is Different

Evansville is Indiana's third-largest city, home to roughly 116,000 residents and anchoring a metro area of about 360,000 people that sits at the center of the Indiana-Kentucky-Illinois Tri-State region. Unlike the white-collar, tech-heavy metros that show up elsewhere in this series, Evansville's economy still runs on making things. Manufacturing is the single largest employment sector across the metro, accounting for more than 33,000 workers, and that figure understates how far the labor pool actually reaches: Toyota Motor Manufacturing Indiana's plant in nearby Princeton employs more than 7,000 people building full-size trucks and SUVs, and BWX Technologies in Mount Vernon employs 500-plus workers manufacturing naval nuclear components under one of the most tightly regulated supply chains in American industry.

As of August 2025, the metro reported roughly 142,500 nonfarm jobs and a 3.6% unemployment rate across more than 8,000 area businesses — a tight labor market by most measures, and one where the recruiting firms that matter most tend to be the ones with existing relationships inside manufacturing plants, skilled-trades unions, and the region's hospital systems, not the ones running a national call center with a Midwest area code bolted on.

That's the backdrop against which this list was built. Custom Staffing Services leans into mining and light-industrial placements. Action Staffing is built around manufacturing, warehouse, and skilled-trades work. HR Solutions occupies the executive-search end of the market that a purely industrial shop doesn't touch. None of that is coincidental — it's what a labor market this manufacturing-heavy actually demands.

1. Custom Staffing Services

Custom Staffing Services has run from 1820 N. Green River Rd., Suites A and B, since it was founded in 1969 as Evansville Employment Services by Sue Ann Noveroske and Martha C. Bowers — 57 years in business by the BBB's own count, making it the longest continuously operating agency on this list by nearly a decade. It has been BBB-accredited since November 1999 and carries an A+ rating, and it also operates satellite offices in Princeton and Hanson, Kentucky, reaching across the river into the Kentucky side of the Tri-State labor market.

The company describes itself on its own site as the "largest locally owned staffing company in the Tri-State area." That's a self-reported claim rather than a figure independently verified for this piece, and it's presented here with that caveat attached. Its actual specialty — mining, light industrial, and clerical/administrative placements — lines up closely with the region's manufacturing and extraction-adjacent employment base.

Best fit for: an employer in mining, light industrial, or clerical/administrative roles that wants the longest-established locally owned firm in the market.

Pricing: custom quote; markup on the hourly bill rate for temp and temp-to-hire, placement fee for direct hire.

2. Action Staffing, Inc.

Action Staffing, Inc. operates from 4004 E. Best fit for: an employer that wants one locally owned generalist covering manufacturing, warehouse, skilled trades, and office roles under a single vendor relationship.

Pricing: custom quote; markup on the hourly bill rate for temp and temp-to-hire placements.

3. HR Solutions, Inc.

HR Solutions, Inc., based at 100 N. St. Joseph Ave., was founded in 1994 — 32 years in business — and has held BBB accreditation with an A+ rating since August 2006. It's led by Vicki Hubiak and holds SBA certification as a Women-Owned Small Business, one of only two firms on this list with a formal ownership certification behind its name.

Unlike the other five entries here, HR Solutions is built around the higher end of the market: executive recruiting, general staffing, corporate outplacement, and executive coaching. Its site cites a "Supplier of the Year / Top Performing Diverse Supplier 2018" recognition. As with Custom Staffing's market-share claim above, this is a self-reported credential rather than one independently confirmed for this piece, and it's noted here as such rather than presented as verified fact.

Best fit for: an employer filling executive or leadership roles, or one that needs outplacement or executive coaching alongside standard staffing.

Pricing: custom quote; placement fee for executive search, markup on the hourly bill rate for general staffing.

Best fit for: an employer that wants light industrial, warehouse, or administrative staffing with the option to scale into direct hire or RPO with the same local firm.

6. Express Employment Professionals — Evansville

Express Employment Professionals is a national franchise brand headquartered in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, but its Evansville office at 1100 Lincoln Ave. is independently, locally owned and operated — confirmed through the BBB, which names Greg Schulten (President/Owner) and Lora Schulten (Owner) directly. The local office has operated since January 1997, with the Schultens' ownership dating to February 2007, putting 29 years of continuous local operation behind the franchise name, and it holds an A+ BBB rating, though it is not BBB-accredited.

Two verification notes are worth disclosing directly. First, the franchise's own website returned a 403 Forbidden error on every attempt to load it through a real headless Chrome browser during this research, not just an automated scraper, so the image above is a Yelp business listing rather than a homepage screenshot — the same wall this series has run into with this specific franchise brand in other cities. Second, that Yelp listing currently shows a 1.8-star rating from five reviews, thin enough that it shouldn't be read as a definitive verdict either way, but it's the kind of thing worth asking about directly rather than assuming away. It's the one entry on this list where a national brand name sits over what is, in practice, a genuinely local, family-run office.

Best fit for: an employer that wants the process infrastructure and name recognition of a national staffing franchise paired with a locally owned, nearly-three-decade Evansville office.

Pricing: custom quote; bill-rate markup for temp and temp-to-hire, placement fee for direct hire.

How This List Was Verified

Roughly twenty-three recruiting and staffing businesses connected to Evansville were reviewed for this piece. Every firm on the final list had to clear the same three checks: a real, staffed office physically located inside Evansville, independently confirmed through at least two of the Better Business Bureau, Google Business Profile, or a comparable public directory; a working means of verifying that address and specialty beyond the company's own marketing; and, where a website existed, content that actually matched the location and service claims rather than a templated regional landing page.

Aerotek, Adecco, Insight Global, TCWGlobal, Surge Staffing, PeopleReady, and ResourceMFG were reviewed and set aside as confirmed corporate branch offices of companies headquartered far outside Evansville — respectively Hanover, Maryland; Switzerland by way of Melville, New York; Atlanta, Georgia; San Diego, California; Ohio; Tacoma, Washington; and Georgia. Each is a legitimate national staffing brand, but none could be tied to named local ownership or leadership the way Express Employment Professionals could, which is the distinction that kept Express on the list despite also being a franchise.

HireLevel has a real Evansville branch that opened in 2013, but the company itself is headquartered in Marion, Illinois, not Evansville. Innovative Staff Solutions runs a seventeen-location corporate network out of Mattoon, Illinois. Malone Staffing Solutions is headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky. All three were excluded on the same local-ownership standard applied above.

A few names fell into a genuine gray area. Holland Sharpe operates across Tennessee, Kentucky, Alabama, and Indiana, but appears corporately based in Tennessee, and no independently verifiable street address distinct from its own marketing page could be confirmed for an Evansville location specifically. Spherion has a franchise model nationally, but no distinct, independently verifiable Evansville-specific office turned up separate from a broader regional territory page. HireQuest Direct lists an Evansville presence, but this research could not independently verify a named local franchisee or owner behind that specific location, the same disclosure standard that kept Aerotek and the other large branches off this list. "Evansville Staffing Solutions" surfaced only inside an AI-generated directory summary, with no independent trace of a real, distinct business by that name in any registry, review platform, or directory checked — it appears to be a hallucinated entry rather than an actual company.

One case deserves its own note rather than a silent omission. Medical Staffing Solutions, LLC, at 9700 Highway 57, Suite A, is a genuinely interesting Evansville story: founded locally in 2001 by nurses Melanie Theriac and Joanie Reneer to serve healthcare and nursing staffing needs, it built a real local track record before being acquired by TotalMed, a national travel-nursing company headquartered in Brookfield, Wisconsin, in August 2019. It kept its original local leadership and office after the acquisition, which makes it a more complicated case than a straightforward corporate branch, but it no longer operates as an independently locally owned business today, which is why it was left off the verified list above rather than included as a local firm.

The six that remain were chosen to cover the ground Evansville's own economy actually demands: the longest-running locally owned generalist, a second locally owned generalist with a broader trades footprint, an executive-search specialist, a locally owned firm with direct hire and RPO capability, a long-running local firm confirmed through directories rather than its own website, and a national franchise brand running as a genuinely local, family-owned office. Figures and details for all six come from each firm's own published pages where available, the Better Business Bureau, and the other public directories named throughout.

For the market-scale figures cited earlier: manufacturing employment and the Toyota and BWX Technologies figures come from the Evansville Regional Economic Partnership's Key Industries page, and the metro's broader economic profile draws on a St. Louis Fed Regional Economist piece on Evansville's economy.

What to Check Before Signing With Any of Them

  • Confirm the current street address before you call — one firm on this list has relocated since older directories were last updated, and several excluded candidates turned out to have no Evansville address at all
  • Ask for the markup percentage or placement fee in writing — none of the six publish rate cards, and quotes vary by role, credential, and contract length
  • Ask directly about any self-reported credential or award — a market-share claim or a supplier award listed on an agency's own site is worth a follow-up question, not an automatic disqualifier
  • For manufacturing or plant-floor roles, ask about safety training and credentialing support — Evansville's largest employers run tightly regulated production lines, and a firm should be able to speak fluently to what that requires
  • Get temp-to-hire conversion terms in writing up front — the fee to bring a temp worker onto your own payroll permanently is where surprises tend to live
  • If you can't verify a company website, look for a consistent address and named owner across multiple public directories — that's the standard this piece used for the one firm here with no independent site

The Bottom Line

Evansville's recruiting market rewards firms that actually understand the Tri-State region's manufacturing, skilled-trades, and healthcare-heavy labor pool over ones running a generic national script. Custom Staffing Services and Action Staffing are the two purely local generalists; HR Solutions covers the executive-search end the others don't touch; JOS Staffing adds direct hire and RPO on top of standard placements; Affinity Staffing has nearly three decades of local history verifiable through directories rather than a website; and Express Employment Professionals shows that a national franchise brand can still be a genuinely local, family-run office once you check who actually owns it.

Whichever you choose, the diligence stays the same: confirm the current address, get the markup or placement fee in writing, and ask directly about anything a firm's own marketing claims rather than taking it at face value.

What's the difference between temp, temp-to-hire, and direct hire staffing in Evansville?

Temp workers stay on the agency's payroll for the length of an assignment. Temp-to-hire lets an employer convert a worker to its own payroll after a trial period, usually for a conversion fee. Direct hire means the agency recruits candidates and the employer hires outright for a one-time placement fee, the model HR Solutions and JOS Staffing both offer alongside standard temp staffing.
