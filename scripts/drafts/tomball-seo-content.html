1. Modern Marketing & Media

Modern Marketing & Media is based at 30703 Raleigh Creek Dr in Tomball, a location independently confirmed across Yelp, the Greater Tomball Area Chamber of Commerce member directory, the Southwest Montgomery County Chamber listing, LinkedIn, and Facebook — five separate sources that all converge on the same address. Founder Amanda Trickey started the agency in 2022, positioning it as a "high-touch," boutique shop for small local businesses rather than a volume-focused agency. Services include a dedicated SEO offering alongside social media management, branding, email marketing, web design, paid ads, and reputation management.

Trickey's local standing is genuinely well-documented: the Southwest Montgomery County Chamber of Commerce named Modern Marketing & Media its 2025 Business of the Year, the Tomball Rotary Club named Trickey its 2025-2026 Citizen of the Year, and she separately founded Tidy Up Tomball, a local community cleanup nonprofit. Voyage Houston Magazine has also profiled her. The agency's own claim of "100+ clients in two years" is worth flagging as self-reported rather than independently audited — a real possibility given the recognition above, but not a figure this piece can verify on its own. Team size and pricing aren't publicly disclosed, and the office runs on an appointment-only schedule, both of which point to a genuinely small, boutique operation rather than a large agency.

Best fit for: a small Tomball-area business that wants a hands-on, boutique relationship with a founder who has deep, verifiable local roots.

Pricing: not published; request a custom quote directly.

That's exactly what a screenshot of its own homepage shows during this review, with a "Houston Creative & Marketing Agency" tag directly in the hero section. The Tomball street address is real and independently confirmed by the city's own Economic Development Corporation and Chamber of Commerce, so the physical presence checks out — it's the company's own marketing positioning, not its street address, that leans on "Houston" rather than "Tomball."

On the numbers: Beefy Marketing carries a 4.7 Google rating across 24 reviews, was featured as a Tomball EDC small-business success story, and has more than 15 years in business. DesignRush, a third-party agency directory, reports an average hourly rate around $199/hr with a minimum project budget in the $1,000-$10,000 range — a data point sourced from DesignRush rather than confirmed directly on Beefy Marketing's own site, so treat it as directionally useful rather than an official rate card.

Best fit for: a business that wants a full-service agency — SEO alongside web design, ads, branding, and AI automation — and doesn't mind that the agency now markets itself under the Houston name despite its real office sitting in Tomball.

Why Tomball's SEO Market Stays Small

The thinness of this list isn't an accident of research; it reflects the actual size of the market. The U.S. Census Bureau's QuickFacts for Tomball puts the city's population at roughly 16,000 to 16,500 as of the 2026 estimate — up an estimated 34% since the 2020 Census, which makes Tomball a genuinely fast-growing suburb, but still a small one by any absolute measure. A city this size, even a fast-growing one, doesn't generate the density of local businesses needed to support many standalone SEO agencies the way a mid-size city would.

The Tomball Economic Development Corporation's own quick-facts data puts the city's business base at roughly 1,500 businesses, about 14,000 employees, and a labor force of 5,880 people. Per Data USA's 2024 figures, educational services, manufacturing, and retail trade are the largest employment sectors in Tomball, and the city's sales tax revenue grew roughly 34% between 2020 and 2022, from $16 million to $21.5 million — real, above-average growth for a suburb this size, but still nowhere near the scale that supports a deep bench of dedicated local SEO shops.

Tomball's one true regional anchor is healthcare: HCA Houston Healthcare Tomball is a 350-bed acute-care hospital with a Level III Trauma Center, an accredited Chest Pain Center, and a Level II NICU, and it has served the region for more than 45 years. That kind of anchor institution draws real regional demand, alongside a median household income of $78,562 as of 2024 per Data USA and Census figures — a solidly middle-class, still-growing exurb rather than either a bedroom community or a corporate-dense suburb like some of its Houston-area neighbors.

Put together, that's a market where most business owners either handle marketing themselves, hire a Houston-metro generalist agency, or work with one of the two verified local options profiled above. It's exactly why the honest count for a "Tomball SEO company" search is two, not six or ten — and why padding this list with Houston-metro landing pages would have made it less useful, not more.

What to Check Before Signing

  • Verify the address is genuine and staffed — search the company name plus its stated street address independently across at least two sources, and ask directly whether the people doing the work sit at that address
  • Confirm SEO is an actual, dedicated service — several genuinely local Tomball businesses in this review turned out to offer PR, social media, or web design without SEO as a confirmed service line
  • Ask exactly how SEO work is delivered — content, technical fixes, and genuine link-building are legitimate; a guaranteed ranking for a flat monthly fee is not
  • Ask to see current client results in a business similar to yours, and press for third-party review evidence beyond an agency's own site-hosted testimonials
  • Clear ownership of your website, Google Business Profile, Analytics, and Search Console — none of it should be held hostage to keep you on a contract
  • A realistic timeline — meaningful SEO movement typically takes three to six months at minimum; anyone promising a specific ranking within weeks is selling, not advising
  • Get pricing in writing upfront — neither company on this list publishes a full rate card, so a scoped, written quote before signing is essential
