Why Deerfield Beach's Recruiting Market Is Different

Deerfield Beach is a city of roughly 86,859 residents at the 2020 census, with a 2024 estimate closer to 90,507, making it the 10th-largest city in Broward County. It doesn't look like a typical beach town on paper: it's headquarters to several sizable private employers, including JM Family Enterprises and its Southeast Toyota Distributors operation, MAPEI Americas, YouFit, and disaster-recovery contractor AshBritt — an unusual concentration of corporate weight for a city this size, and one that shapes what kind of recruiting actually gets done here.

At the same time, the city's income profile sits below the county around it. Median household income for 2017-2021 (American Community Survey estimates) was $49,650, notably under Broward County's $64,522, and the poverty rate was 15.6% against the county's 12.4%. Deerfield Beach also has historically significant Brazilian and Haitian immigrant communities, which matters directly for staffing demand in light industrial, hospitality, and general-labor roles — the kind of work a full-service agency like Employer Solutions Resources is built around.

Put those pieces together — one very large corporate HQ employer, a median income below the surrounding county, and a diverse immigrant workforce — and it explains why the six agencies that actually verified out here don't cluster into one category. All Star Healthcare Solutions and Onward Healthcare cover physician and allied-health staffing for the region's hospital systems. Florida Construction Connection and KRS Recruiting Agency work the construction-management and legal niches that a growth market like this generates. Employer Solutions Resources covers the light-industrial, hospitality, and logistics roles that the local economy and workforce actually need filled day to day. TBG's Southeast branch handles the corporate, accounting, and finance searches that JM Family and its peers create. That spread, not a single dominant staffing category, is the real shape of Deerfield Beach's hiring market.

1. All Star Healthcare Solutions

All Star Healthcare Solutions — still reachable at its long-running allstarrecruiting.com domain alongside its newer allstarhealthcaresolutions.com branding — has operated from 800 Fairway Dr, Suite 300, since it was founded on May 1, 2003, putting 23 years behind it. It carries a BBB A+ rating, though it is not BBB-accredited, and independent data providers place its headcount above 200 employees, a meaningful size for a firm working a single staffing specialty.

That specialty is locum tenens: temporary and contract placements for physicians and advanced-practice providers filling coverage gaps at hospitals and health systems. Two credentials on the company's own materials are worth disclosing rather than repeating at face value. PR Newswire named it one of the "2019 Largest Locum Tenens Staffing Firms in the U.S." — a real press mention, but a seven-year-old one, not a current ranking. It also lists a 2025 "Best of Staffing" award, a ClearlyRated-sourced recognition program that a large share of the staffing industry participates in and that reflects client/candidate satisfaction survey results rather than an independently audited market-share claim. Both are noted here as legitimate but self-reported or dated credentials, not disqualifiers.

Best fit for: a hospital or health system that needs physician or advanced-practitioner locum tenens coverage from a firm with two decades of specialty focus.

2. Florida Construction Connection, Inc. (FLCC)

Florida Construction Connection operates from 10 Fairway Dr, Suite 303, and has done so since it was founded on February 2, 2004 — about 21 years in business. Its footprint is smaller than the healthcare firms on this list, with roughly 15 employees per RocketReach, but its niche is a distinctive one: construction-management career coaching and recruiting built around its own trademarked "The Breistol Method," serving South Florida's construction and real-estate development sector specifically rather than staffing generally.

It holds a BBB A+ rating (not accredited, file opened in 2017) and carries more than 14,400 LinkedIn followers, a sizable audience for a firm this size. One address discrepancy is worth flagging directly: a Yelp listing shows a different address, "1201 SE 15th Ave." Five independent, mutually consistent sources — the company's own site, its BBB file, its LinkedIn page, RocketReach, and a SHRM directory listing, alongside its Google Business Profile — all confirm 10 Fairway Dr. as current, so the Yelp entry appears to be stale rather than a second active location.

Best fit for: a South Florida construction, engineering, or real-estate development employer that wants a recruiter fluent in the industry's own career-path structure, not a generalist.

Pricing: custom quote; construction-management search and coaching is typically priced as a placement fee rather than an hourly bill-rate markup.

3. Employer Solutions Resources (ESR)

Employer Solutions Resources, based at 217 Goolsby Blvd, describes itself as a full-service staffing, payroll, and PEO firm serving the Southeastern United States, with ZoomInfo placing headcount between 201 and 500 employees across a multi-state operation. Its specialty runs wide: manufacturing, hospitality and event staffing, forklift and logistics roles, administrative and professional placements, housekeeping, and call-center staffing — the generalist, light-industrial-and-hospitality footprint this series has found tends to show up wherever a diverse, lower-median-income workforce needs filling fast.

One discrepancy deserves a direct callout rather than a footnote. ESR's own materials state "over 25 years" servicing the Southeast, but its Google Business Profile currently shows "5+ years." The most likely explanation is that the operating brand is genuinely older while the current LLC entity behind it was filed more recently — a common pattern when a staffing business restructures or rebrands its legal entity without actually being a new operation. It's presented here as a discrepancy worth asking about directly, not as evidence the company is misrepresenting itself.

Best fit for: a Deerfield Beach-area employer with recurring manufacturing, hospitality, logistics, or administrative staffing needs that wants one vendor handling placement and payroll together.

Pricing: custom quote; staffing priced as a bill-rate markup, with PEO-style payroll services typically priced as a percentage of payroll.

What's the difference between locum tenens, travel nursing, and direct-hire staffing?

Direct hire means the agency recruits candidates for a permanent role and the employer hires outright for a one-time placement fee.
