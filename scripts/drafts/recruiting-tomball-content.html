What to Check Before Hiring a Staffing Agency

  • Confirm the current address by phone — both companies in this review have an address detail worth double-checking directly rather than trusting a single online listing
  • Ask whether the agency does staffing or home health care — Tomball's healthcare-anchored economy means genuine staffing agencies and licensed home health providers sometimes share search results and BBB categories; they are not the same service
  • Verify the website actually loads before you rely on it — a business can be real and still have an unreachable, expired, or misconfigured website; don't assume a domain that resolves is necessarily the right one
  • Ask exactly which placement types are offered — temporary, temp-to-hire, and direct-hire are different arrangements with different fee structures, and not every firm offers all three
  • Check BBB accreditation status directly — "not accredited" or "not rated" isn't automatically disqualifying for a small or newer business, but it's a fact worth knowing before signing
  • Get pricing and placement-guarantee terms in writing upfront — neither firm on this list publishes a rate card, so a scoped, written quote before signing is essential
  • Ask for recent, checkable references in a role or industry similar to yours, rather than relying solely on directory listings or self-reported awards
