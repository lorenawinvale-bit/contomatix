Google's link spam examples, and the white hat answer

Examples quoted from the link spam section of Google Search Central spam policies, read on 23 October 2026. The right column is our reading.

Google's example of link spamWhite hat answer
Buying or selling links for ranking purposesDo not; qualify paid placements as sponsored
Excessive link exchanges and cross-link partner pagesLink only where it helps readers
Automated programs or services creating linksAvoid completely
Low-quality directory or bookmark site linksUse few, relevant listings
Optimized anchors in guest posts or press releasesUse natural, brand or descriptive anchors
Widely distributed footer or widget linksDo not distribute keyword links
