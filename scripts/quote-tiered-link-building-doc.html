Google Search Central · Spam policies, link spam

The examples that sit under every link tier

Link spam is the practice of creating links to or from a site primarily for the purpose of manipulating search rankings. Examples include using automated programs or services to create links to your site, low-quality directory or bookmark site links, and keyword-rich, hidden, or low-quality links embedded in widgets that are distributed across various sites.

Google does understand that buying and selling links is a normal part of the economy of the web for advertising and sponsorship purposes, as long as they are qualified with rel="nofollow" or rel="sponsored".
