# Brief: "[Industry] SEO" posts for Contomatix (read fully before writing)

You are writing new blog posts for Contomatix, a live white-hat SEO/link-building agency site (Express + EJS, merges to `main` auto-deploy). The owner has approved this plan. You may write and open a PR without further confirmation. Do NOT push to `main` and do NOT merge: work on your own branch and open a pull request; a human reviews every post before it goes live.

## Repo orientation
- Posts live in one JSON array, `data/blog.json`. Post fields: `slug`, `title`, `category`, `author`, `date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `heroDiagram`, `excerpt` (<=160 chars, used as meta description), `content` (HTML string). Do NOT set an `image` field (a human designs cover illustrations afterwards; reusing another post's image caused a real bug before).
- `lib/blogStore.js` `getPublished()` hides posts whose `date` is in the future and publishes them automatically on that date. Use EXACTLY the date given for each post in your batch.
- Run `node scripts/site-audit-full.js` before and after your changes. Error and warning counts must not increase. (Expect the `image` missing errors to rise by one per new post: that is the known, accepted cost of leaving `image` unset. Everything else must stay flat.)
- Category: `"Local SEO"` for local-service industries; `"On-Page SEO"` for the bold-keywords post.
- Reference posts to match tone and structure conventions (do not copy their structure): `seo-for-dentists-2026`, `how-to-get-cited-by-chatgpt-2026`, `ai-seo-strategy`.
- FAQ block near the end of `content`, exact pattern: `<div class="post-faq"><details><summary>Question?</summary><p>Answer.</p></details>...</div>`. 5-8 entries built from real question-style search phrasing for that topic.
- External links: `<a href="..." target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">`. NEVER put an external link in the first 10% of the `content` string. Put source citations near the end as a "Sources:" line if you cite any.
- Length: 1800-2500 words, `<h2>` sections. Start the content with `<p class="article-updated" style="color: #6b6459; font-size: 0.85rem; font-style: italic; margin: -8px 0 20px;">Last updated: September 2026</p>` like other posts do.
- Title: 50-65 characters, keyword first. Put the focus keyword in the first two sentences and in at least one `<h2>`. Use the given slug exactly.

## Mandatory hero diagram for every post
Every post has a `heroDiagram` (SVG next to the H1), generated by the repo script. For each post: pick a style (`wheel`, `timeline`, or `ring`; vary them within your batch), hand-pick 4-5 short labels (2-5 words) from that post's real `<h2>` headings, then run:
`node scripts/generate-topic-diagram.js public/images/blog/diagram-<slug>.svg <style> "<short topic title>" "<label1>" "<label2>" "<label3>" "<label4>"`
and set `"heroDiagram": "/images/blog/diagram-<slug>.svg"`. Slugs here are like `seo-for-nursing-homes`, so the file is `diagram-seo-for-nursing-homes.svg`.

## Hard content rules (breaking any of these is worse than shipping nothing)
1. NO fabricated statistics, prices, percentages, counts, or "studies". If you cannot verify a number from a source you actually retrieved, describe the point qualitatively instead. If WebFetch is blocked in your sandbox (it often is), do not cite a number from that source anyway. Never attach a specific number to a real-looking link you could not open.
2. NO invented experience. Contomatix has NOT run campaigns for these industries. Never write "we helped a nursing home...", "our client...", "in our tests...", or invent a case study, quote, testimonial or before/after result. Write as an informed guide: "here is what to check", "here is how this tends to work", not "here is what we did for X". The bold-keywords post especially must not claim any test was run.
3. NO naming or criticising any competing SEO agency or SaaS product. Naming Google products (Google Business Profile, Search Console, Rich Results Test, PageSpeed Insights), government/regulator sites and neutral public directories where the reader genuinely needs them is fine.
4. NO city-listicle format ("Top N ... in [City]"). These are industry how-to guides.
5. Each post must be genuinely different in structure and content, driven by that industry's real quirks (who the buyer is, how they decide, urgency vs research, regulation/advertising rules, seasonality, how reviews and categories work). Do NOT reuse a shared template of identical H2s across posts, and avoid repeated boilerplate paragraphs between posts. The site was previously hurt by templated near-duplicate posts. Every section should say something a reader could not have guessed. Prefer one concrete, specific, correct explanation over three generic bullets.
6. Regulated or sensitive industries (healthcare, counseling, law, funeral, senior care): state clearly where advertising/privacy rules apply (e.g. patient privacy, state bar advertising rules, licensing-board rules) without giving legal advice, and tell the reader to check their own regulator. Be respectful in tone for funeral and senior-care topics.
7. Do not mention search volume, keyword difficulty or any internal keyword-research numbers.
8. Working titles in the plan that promise something unverifiable ("What Testing Shows", "The Proven Local Playbook") must be reworded honestly, e.g. "Do Bold Keywords Help SEO? What Google Has Actually Said" and "Pest Control SEO: A Local Playbook".

## Internal links
2-4 per post, only to things that exist right now: the service pages `/services/on-page-seo`, `/services/link-building`, `/services/keyword-research`, `/services/technical-seo`, `/services/ai-seo`, and genuinely relevant existing posts already in `data/blog.json` (check the slug exists). Do NOT link to other posts in this plan (some are dated later and would 404 until they publish). Contomatix has no "local SEO" service page, so do not invent one.

## Workflow
1. `git checkout -b <branch given in your batch>`
2. Read 2 reference posts, run the audit, note baseline counts.
3. Write each post, add to `data/blog.json` (append at the end), generate each hero diagram. Validate with `node -e "require('./data/blog.json')"`.
4. Re-run the audit; compare with baseline (only the +1 `image` error per post is acceptable).
5. Commit, push the branch, open a PR against `main` with a summary listing each post (slug, date, author, word count, sources cited). Do not merge.
If the repo looks different from this description, adapt, but never relax the hard content rules.
