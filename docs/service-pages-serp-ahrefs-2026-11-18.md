Observations
- Top results for service queries are mostly vendor landing pages, 1,200 to 5,000 words; our pages are 2,200 to 3,000.
- Several top results have 0 to 22 backlinks (on-page and off-page), so on-page quality can compete there.
- On-page AI Overview lists: content/keyword optimization, content gaps, titles and meta, heading structure, internal and external links, schema, image names and alt text, page speed.
- Off-page AI Overview lists: link building, backlink audits and disavow, digital PR, citations, brand mentions and social signals.
- White-label AI Overview lists: client agreement, invisible fulfilment, branded delivery; inclusions (technical, on-page, link building, dashboards); benefits (scale, margin, ownership).
- Guest-posting top pages show vetting criteria, a 3-step process, turnaround, a guarantee and "starts at" prices. We publish no prices or guarantees.
- AI SEO: Ahrefs question "which seo agencies offer top ai seo services" is 3.9K; PAA style queries about agencies.
- Technical SEO: "technical seo audit services" 2.0K; sources note audit prices usually exclude fixes.
- Google blocked further searches (429 captcha) after 4 queries; the keyword-research, AI SEO and technical SERPs were read through web search summaries, not Google's AI Overview.
