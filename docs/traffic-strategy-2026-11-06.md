Goal set by the team: 1,000 organic clicks a month. Today the site is about 4 months old, has about 375 posts and 42 free tools, and gets about 63 clicks in 28 days.

## 2. What the numbers say

1. **The problem is not visibility, it is rank and authority.** 79K impressions at CTR 0.1% means we show up on page 5 and below. Average position 52.8 is not a content-volume problem.
2. **Ranking top 10 is not enough when the queries are tiny.** Our 74 top-10 pages sit on queries with almost no demand. To get 1,000 clicks we need queries with real volume, not more 10 to 50 searches a month terms.
3. **More posts have not moved traffic.** 375 posts, and about half the pages have under 50 impressions in three months. Publishing 3 posts a day is now the least useful hour of the day.
4. **We have very little authority.** 38 links, two-thirds to the home page, over half from two directories. Our best pages (position 60 to 85) are link-poor.
5. **Where we do get links and near-page-one rankings is AI search and tools.** `llm-seo-checker` has 4 links, `llms-txt-generator` 2, and `how-duckduckgo-ai-search-works-2026` ranks 8.0 with 682 impressions. The city lists give impressions but have not earned links.
6. **The city listicles are our only volume and our biggest risk.** 74% of impressions come from a format Google may treat as scaled content, and our two other sites saw impressions collapse in late September (one has since recovered).

## 3. Target and honest timeline

These are targets to steer by, not forecasts. We have 38 links and a young domain, so reaching 1,000 clicks a month is a 6 to 9 month goal, and only if the authority work below actually happens.

| Month | Real organic clicks (28 days, excluding Pakistan) | Referring domains pointing to inner pages |
|---|---|---|
| Now | about 50 | not measured (13 of 38 links point to inner pages) |
| End of Dec | 150 | 15 |

A rough check: to get 1,000 clicks at about 4% CTR (a common estimate for positions 4 to 6, not our own number) we need about 25,000 impressions a month in the top 6. Today we have about 79,000 impressions but almost all below position 30.

## 4. Strategy in five moves

### Move 1: Authority first (the main bottleneck)
- Goal: 20 new referring domains in 60 days, pointing at inner pages, not the home page.
- Link targets (in order): the AI search tools (`llm-seo-checker`, `llms-txt-generator`), our data studies (canonical scan, meta description lengths, subdomain split, content gap check), and the 10 pages in Move 3.
- Methods: tool and data outreach to blogs and newsletters that write about the topic ("we scanned 369 ranking pages", "we measured 363 posts"), resource-page inclusions, guest posts with a relevant anchor, and digital PR on the AI search angle.
- Rules: relevance over count, varied anchors, no link exchanges or networks. Track each link: date, source, target page, anchor.
- Stop counting directory listings as progress. 21 of our 38 links are two directories.

### Move 2: Fewer, bigger pages around topics with real volume
- Stop writing posts for terms under about 100 searches a month. Pick topics with 500+ a month and low difficulty where we have first-hand data.
- Examples from our own keyword pulls this week (US, KD 15 or lower): long tail keywords 3.7K, what is a subdomain 3.3K, canonical url 2.1K, subdomain seo 1.4K, seo meta description length 1.0K.
- Week 1 job: pull 40 head terms with volume 500+ and KD 10 or lower around our topics (technical SEO, AI search, schema, sitemaps, robots.txt, link building) from Ahrefs. The mirror was not loading on 6 Nov, so this list is not done yet.
- Each pick gets one page, one owner and one tracked keyword. Check cannibalization first.
- New output rate: 1 deep post a day at most, 3 a week is fine. Spend the freed time on Moves 1 and 3.

### Move 3: Push the pages that are already close
- 58 pages sit at position 8 to 20 with 30+ impressions. Take the top 15 and for each one: tighten title and H1 to the query, add first-hand data, add 5+ internal links from related posts, and give it one real external link.
- Start with: `how-duckduckgo-ai-search-works-2026` (position 8.0, 682 impressions), `innovative-marketing-campaigns-2026` (10.3, 989), `digital-marketing-agency-mexico-2026` (13.9, 1,347), `moses-lake-search-engine-optimization-2026` (10.4, 1,339), `top-seo-companies-pleasanton-2026` (16.8, 831), `best-local-seo-company-brentwood-2026` (17.8, 807), `hreflang-spanish` (16.8, 295), plus the Conroe, Renton, Tomball, Sugar Land and Pasadena lists.
- Check each week: position and impressions for the target query, not total clicks.

### Move 4: Double down on AI search, our one real edge
- It is the only cluster where we already earn links and rankings, and it matches the hub-and-spoke plan from September (AI SEO as the core topic, AEO, GEO, LLMO and AI search around it).
- Build: a pillar page for "AI SEO", 8 to 10 explainers in the style of the DuckDuckGo post, a study of our own (for example, which AI crawlers robots.txt files block across 300 sites), and keep the llms.txt and LLM checker tools updated.
- Every page in this cluster links to the pillar and the tools.

### Move 5: Stabilise the city listicles
- Do not add more. Do not delete yet.
- Refresh the 20 with the most impressions using verified details (real addresses, checked sources) and internal links.
- After 60 days, review the pages with under 10 impressions (85 pages) and decide merge, improve or noindex, using the data then.

## 5. 90-day plan

| Weeks | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup | Ahrefs list of 40 head terms, link tracking sheet, exclude internal traffic in reporting, pick the 10 link-magnet pages |
| 2 to 4 | Authority and quick wins | 8 new referring domains, Move 3 refresh on 15 pages, AI search pillar page started |
| 5 to 8 | Build | 4 data-led posts on real-volume topics, AI search study published and pitched, 12 more referring domains |
| 9 to 12 | Measure and repeat | Month-end export, compare with the 28 Oct baseline, double what worked, drop what did not |

## 6. What to measure (weekly, in Search Console and the link sheet)

1. Real organic clicks per 28 days, excluding Pakistan and the home page.
2. Referring domains, and how many point to inner pages.
3. Pages with 100+ impressions at position 10 or better.
4. Position and impressions of the 15 Move 3 pages and the 10 Move 2 pages.
5. Leads (contact form, WhatsApp, calls), because 1,000 clicks only matters if some of them become clients.

## 6. Risks

- Authority work is slow and Google may not respond for 2 to 3 months. Do not judge a link inside 2 weeks.
- The city listicles are 74% of impressions. A core update could cut them, so the new pages must not depend on them.
- Our two other sites saw impressions collapse in late September. Treat the quality of every new page as the first defence.
